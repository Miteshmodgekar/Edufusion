// Modules

Everything the department runs on, connected.

Five modules, one shared source of truth — a change in one shows up everywhere else instantly.

Live Attendance

Reads directly from the Excel register faculty update — students see their real percentage the moment the sheet changes.

AI Risk Prediction

Scores every student's academic risk from attendance and marks trends, flagging who needs support before it's too late.

Placement Eligibility

Checks CGPA, backlogs and live attendance against each drive's criteria, and matches students to roles by skill overlap.

Leave Workflow

Routes a request from mentor to HOD automatically, with status visible to the student at every step.

Notifications

Low-attendance and risk alerts land in-app and by email the moment they're detected — nothing sits in a spreadsheet unseen.
